# Warranty Policy

## Coverage Overview

ShopEase offers warranty support on eligible products purchased from the official store. Warranty coverage varies by product category and manufacturer policy and is intended to protect against manufacturing defects and material faults under normal use.

## Warranty Period

Most products are covered by a manufacturer warranty for a period ranging from 6 months to 24 months from the date of delivery. The exact term is listed on the product page or in the order confirmation details.

## What Is Covered

Warranty coverage generally includes:

- Manufacturing defects
- Faulty materials or assembly issues
- Functional defects that occur under normal product usage

## What Is Not Covered

Warranty does not normally cover:

- Normal wear and tear
- Accidental damage or mishandling
- Water damage, impact damage, or misuse
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality
- Damage caused by natural disasters or external incidents

## Warranty Claim Process

To initiate a warranty claim, customers should contact support with the order number, product details, purchase date, and a description of the issue. Supporting photos or videos may be required for assessment.

## Resolution Options

Depending on the issue and product type, ShopEase or the manufacturer may offer one of the following:

- repair
- replacement parts
- exchange for a similar product
- refund or store credit if the product is deemed non-repairable

## Exclusions and Limits

Products sold as used, refurbished, or clearance items may have different or limited warranty coverage. Some accessories, consumables, and software products may not qualify for full warranty protection.

## Support Contact

Customers with warranty concerns should reach out to support with their order information and product details for case review and next steps.
