# ShopEase Return Policy

## Return Window

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days from the date the order was delivered. The return request must be initiated before the return window closes.

## Eligibility

Products must:

- Be unused and in a resalable condition
- Be in their original packaging when possible
- Include all accessories, manuals, and included items
- Not show signs of wear, damage, or misuse

## Non-Returnable Products

The following products cannot normally be returned:

- Personalized or custom-made items
- Digital products and downloadable content
- Gift cards and vouchers
- Final sale or clearance products
- Items that have been used extensively or damaged by the customer

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

Customers must report damaged, defective, or incorrect products within 48 hours of delivery. A photo or video evidence may be required to validate the claim.

## Return Shipping

For defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped products, ShopEase covers the return shipping cost. For change-of-mind returns, the customer is generally responsible for return shipping unless otherwise stated in the order or promotional terms.

## Return Process

1. Contact support with the order number and issue details.
2. Receive a return authorization if the item qualifies.
3. Pack the item securely and use the provided return label when applicable.
4. Ship the item to the designated returns center.
5. Wait for the item to be received and inspected before final processing.

## Refund After Return

Once the returned item is inspected and approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method. Refund processing begins after inspection and may take up to 10 business days depending on the payment provider.

## Exchanges

If a product is defective or incorrect, customers may be eligible for an exchange or replacement depending on stock availability and policy approval. Exchanges are handled separately from standard returns.
