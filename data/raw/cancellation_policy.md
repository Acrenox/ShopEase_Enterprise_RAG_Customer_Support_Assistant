# Cancellation Policy

## Overview

ShopEase allows customers to cancel eligible orders before they are packed and dispatched. Orders that have entered the shipping process, or products that are custom-made, personalized, or marked final sale, may not be eligible for cancellation.

## Cancellation Window

Customers may request a cancellation in the following cases:

- Before the order is packed for dispatch
- Before the courier has picked up the package
- Before a custom or personalized order enters production

Once the order is marked as shipped, the cancellation request is generally not accepted.

## How to Request a Cancellation

Customers can request cancellation through the order details page in the account dashboard or by contacting support with their order number and email address used at checkout. Requests are reviewed in order of receipt.

## Refunds for Cancelled Orders

If a cancellation is approved before dispatch, the order value is normally refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. If the order was partially processed or an item was already shipped, the refund may be reduced to cover shipping or handling charges.

## Non-Cancellable Items

The following orders may not be cancelled:

- Items already shipped or delivered
- Personalized, engraved, or custom-made products
- Gift cards and digital codes
- Products marked as final sale
- Orders already covered by a return or exchange request

## Exceptions and Special Cases

For high-priority or time-sensitive orders, support may review a cancellation request on a case-by-case basis. If an order has already left the warehouse, the customer may need to wait for delivery and then initiate a return under the return policy.

## Customer Support

If a customer is unsure whether an order can still be cancelled, they should contact support with the order number and shipping status for a quick review.
