# Refund Policy

## Overview

ShopEase issues refunds only for eligible returns, cancelled orders, or approved payment disputes. Refunds are processed after the returned item is received, verified, and inspected.

## Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are normally reflected in the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the returned item is received and checked. In some cases, banks or payment providers may take additional time to post the refund.

## Eligibility

Refunds are available when:

- the return is approved under the return policy
- the item is unused, in original condition, and includes packaging and accessories
- the order qualifies under the cancellation policy before dispatch
- the item is not excluded by policy, such as personalized or digital products

## Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally not eligible for refund:

- Digital products and downloadable content
- Gift cards and vouchers
- Customized, engraved, or personalized products
- Final sale items
- Used products that do not meet return conditions

## Deductions

Refund amounts may be reduced to account for shipping fees, restocking charges, or product condition issues. If an item is damaged, missing accessories, or returned outside policy, the claim may be reviewed and a partial refund may be offered.

## Payment Method

Refunds are usually issued to the original payment method used at checkout. If the original method is unavailable or expired, support may guide the customer on alternate processing options.

## Refund Disputes

If a customer disputes a charge or believes the refund was processed incorrectly, they should contact support with the order number, payment reference, and the reason for the dispute. ShopEase may request proof of delivery, tracking details, or product condition photos when needed.
