# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Timeframes

Orders are typically delivered within 5 to 7 business days for standard shipping in India. Delivery times may vary depending on the destination, courier partner, and high-demand periods. Express shipping options may be available at checkout for eligible products.

## Tracking and Updates

Once an order is dispatched, a tracking link is sent by email or can be accessed through the customer account dashboard. Customers can use the order number and email address to check the current shipment status.

## Delayed, Lost, or Missing Shipments

If a package is delayed, appears lost, or has not arrived within the expected delivery window, customers should contact support with the order number and the email used during checkout. The support team may request additional verification before arranging a replacement or refund.

## Shipping Charges

Shipping charges depend on the order value, delivery region, selected shipping method, and product weight. Free shipping is available on eligible orders above the minimum threshold, and some promotional events may include shipping discounts.

## Order Processing Time

Orders are usually processed within 1 to 2 business days before dispatch. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may take longer to be packed and handed over to the courier.

## Address and Delivery Issues

Customers are responsible for ensuring the delivery address is correct at checkout. If the address is incomplete or incorrect, the order may be delayed or returned to sender. Additional charges may apply for redelivery or address corrections.

## International Shipping

International orders may be subject to customs clearance, duties, or taxes depending on the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the customer unless otherwise stated at checkout.

## Shipment Exceptions

Some products may be excluded from certain shipping methods due to weight, size, or local restrictions. In such cases, support will communicate the available options and expected delivery timelines.
