# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, a tracking link is sent by email. You can also view the tracking status from your account dashboard using the order number and email address used at checkout.

## Can I cancel my order after it has been shipped?

Usually no. Orders can only be cancelled before they are packed or handed to the courier. After the order is shipped, customers should wait for delivery and then follow the return process if needed.

## What if my item arrives damaged?

Customers should report damaged or defective items within 48 hours of delivery. Support may ask for photos and order details before approving the return or replacement.

## How long does a refund take?

Approved refunds usually appear in the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the return is received and inspected.

## Can I return an item that I changed my mind about?

Yes, if the item meets the return conditions and is within the return window. Change-of-mind returns may require the customer to pay return shipping unless the product is faulty or incorrect.

## Do you offer free shipping?

Free shipping may be available on eligible orders above a set minimum value. Shipping charges may also vary by region and product size.

## What products are not eligible for returns?

Custom-made items, digital products, gift cards, personalized goods, and final sale items are generally not returnable unless there is a product defect or fulfilment error.

## How do I file a warranty claim?

Contact support with the order number and item details. Provide a description of the issue and any photos or videos needed to confirm the fault.

## Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?

Yes, exchanges may be approved for defective, wrong, or damaged products depending on stock availability. The support team can guide the customer on available replacement options.

## What if I entered the wrong address?

If the order has not yet been shipped, support may be able to update the address. Once the parcel is in transit, address changes may not be possible and a redelivery may be needed.
