# Product Information

## Product Categories

ShopEase sells a range of consumer products across electronics, home essentials, accessories, lifestyle items, and personal-use goods. Each product page includes essential information such as price, delivery time, warranty details, and return eligibility.

## Product Conditions and Availability

Products may be listed as:

- New and sealed
- Open-box or lightly used
- Refurbished
- Final sale
- Personalized or custom-made

Customers should review the item description before purchase, especially for return eligibility and warranty terms.

## Recommended Product Care

For most products, customers are encouraged to keep original packaging until they confirm the item is working as expected. This helps with returns, exchanges, or warranty claims if an issue is reported after delivery.

## Accessories and Included Items

Many products include standard accessories such as charging cables, adapters, manuals, and protective packaging. Items sold without accessories are clearly marked on the product page.

## Personalized and Custom Products

Products that are personalized, customized, engraved, or made-to-order may not be eligible for standard returns. These items are usually excluded from the regular refund and cancellation policies unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

## Final Sale and Clearance Items

Final sale products are not usually refundable or returnable unless there is a defect, incorrect shipment, or other protected issue covered by policy. Customers should read product conditions carefully before placing the order.

## Support for Product Issues

If a product is defective, missing components, or delivered in a damaged condition, customers should contact support and provide proof of the issue. ShopEase may request photos, video, or order details to verify the claim.

## Warranty Notes

Eligible products come with a warranty period that varies by brand and category. Warranty support is typically provided for manufacturing faults under normal use and not for accidental or cosmetic damage.
